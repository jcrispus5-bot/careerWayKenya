Career Resources

Get practical advice to help you prepare for opportunities, communicate your strengths, and grow professionally.

Job applications

How to Write a Strong CV

Present your experience, skills, education, and projects clearly and professionally.

Read practical tips
  • Use clear headings and consistent formatting.
  • Tailor your CV to the role you want.
  • Highlight relevant projects and measurable results.
  • Check spelling, contact details, and dates.

Interview preparation

Prepare for Interviews

Learn to answer questions confidently and explain why you're suitable for a position.

Read practical tips
  • Research the organization and the role.
  • Practice explaining your experience with examples.
  • Use the STAR method for behavioral questions.
  • Prepare thoughtful questions for the interviewer.

Practical experience

Build Your Portfolio

Show employers what you can do through real projects and clear explanations of your work.

Read practical tips
  • Choose projects relevant to your target career.
  • Explain the problem, process, and outcome.
  • Include screenshots, demos, or sample work.
  • Keep links updated and easy to navigate.

Skill development

Learn New Skills

Create a realistic study routine and turn learning into practical experience.

Read practical tips
  • Choose one clear learning goal at a time.
  • Use structured tutorials and courses.
  • Practice through projects rather than watching alone.
  • Review progress regularly and adjust your plan.

Career planning

Set Career Goals

Break a long-term ambition into smaller, achievable milestones.

Read practical tips
  • Identify your interests and current strengths.
  • Research skills required for your target role.
  • Set deadlines for specific milestones.
  • Track progress and celebrate achievements.

Professional growth

Grow Your Network

Build professional relationships and discover opportunities to learn from others.

Read practical tips
  • Connect with professionals in your field.
  • Join relevant communities and events.
  • Ask specific questions and offer help when possible.
  • Maintain relationships respectfully.
